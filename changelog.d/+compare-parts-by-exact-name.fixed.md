Comparing two decks (`verify_circuit`'s comparison, the export's `diff_vs_prior` and `edit_schematic`'s reference check) pairs parts by their exact reference first, so `RLoad` and `R§Load` in one deck are two parts and each change is reported under the reference as written. A part pairs by its name without the section sign only when it has no exact partner; when that would pair more than one part on a side, the comparison names them under `components_ambiguous` (`ambiguous` in the equivalence report) and is not `equivalent`.
