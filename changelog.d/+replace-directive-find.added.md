`edit_schematic`'s `replace_directive` takes `find`: the words to change, as the text reads, found exactly once in the directive or comment `instruction` names, the texts at `x` and `y`, or else every text on the sheet. Only those words are replaced, so one `.param` in a block of several changes without retyping the block. The result gives the first `line` touched and only the touched lines as `was` and `now`. A `find` found nowhere, in several texts or several times in one text is refused, with the texts or places it found.
