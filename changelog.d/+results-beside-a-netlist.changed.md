`execution.results_beside_sheet` and `jobs(action="place_beside_sheet")` now take a netlist as they take a sheet: the run's results and log are copied beside the netlist under its name, where LTspice's own batch run writes them. A job whose circuits would put two runs into one results file (`foo.asc` and `foo.net`, or one circuit named twice) is refused, and so is a netlist with a sheet of its name beside it, whose results file is that sheet's.
