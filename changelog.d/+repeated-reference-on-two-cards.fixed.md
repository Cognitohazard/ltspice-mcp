A netlist that gives one reference to more than one card of one scope (`R1` twice at the top level, or twice in one `.subckt` body, compared without case) no longer loses a card. `inspect`'s components and net queries list every card and name the repeat in `repeated_references`, and a net query at a pin of such a reference is refused naming its lines. `verify_circuit` reports it as `shared_reference`, an `equivalence` comparison names it and is not equivalent, a `structural_diff` leaves it uncompared with `equivalent` null, `run_experiments` lints it as `repeated-reference`, and Monte Carlo mismatch leaves such a transistor out as unsized instead of drawing for one of its cards.
