After a run, a `saves_not_stored` observation names each `.save` entry the results do not hold, with why (another LTspice build's spelling of a path, an ngspice `.save` of a colon path, a node voltage the raw lacks), on the receipt and on every later read of the run by job. An entry whose stored name is not established (a pattern such as `V(x1.*)`, a current that is not stored under the `.save`'s own spelling) is listed as not checked rather than guessed. A deck with no `.save` card gets no such observation.
