The statistics of a `.meas` measurement (the `measurements` recipe's `stats`, and the Python API's `compute_measurement_stats`) counted every step with no number in `failure_count`. `failure_count` now counts only the steps the simulator printed failed. Each other step with no number is counted in the new `no_number` object, under the name of the row field that gives its reason: `unreadable`, `answer_unknown` or `not_established`. A null in a caller's own list that comes with no reason is counted as `reason_not_given`. `total_count` is `valid_count` plus `failure_count` plus the counts in `no_number`. On LTspice XVII, a step of a stepped table printed as 0 is counted as `not_established`, which makes `failure_count` a lower bound. `compute_measurement_stats` takes the reasons in a new `reasons` argument, and the Python API's new `no_number_reasons(table, name)` gives them for a measurement of the table `measurements()` returns.
