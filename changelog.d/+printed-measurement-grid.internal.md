The recorded LTspice behaviour of printed measurements is indexed as a grid in `cases.toml`: each `.meas` form by analysis, stepping and outcome, apart from the measurement's name, a WHEN's level, the complex format and the card that printed it. One test reads every recorded cell through every route that reads a measurement, an integrity test holds the index to its decks, and decks for the cells no recording fills wait for the next recording run.
