A slow write of a launch record no longer fails another case of a job run with `execution.recoverable`, and a cancel from another server process no longer waits behind a launch. Each launch held the job's cancellation lock while its launch record was written, so the job's other launches waited for that write and failed with `submission_failed` after the lock's ten-second timeout, and a cancel from another process waited behind it and could time out. A launch now holds the lock only to decide whether it may start.
