A Monte Carlo receipt says when its samples computed the same results. Each
produced case keeps a digest of what its raw computed, every plot's variables
and data and not the date in its headers, and samples of one circuit that
share a digest are listed in an `identical_samples` observation, which leads
with "All samples of …" when no sample differed. Their draws changed nothing
the simulator saved: a rule that reaches no part the deck saves, or a PDK whose
statistical models never switched on. A sample with no digest, recorded before
this version or with a raw that cannot be read that way, is reported in a
`samples_not_compared` observation instead of being left out.
