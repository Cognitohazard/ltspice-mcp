A Monte Carlo receipt says where its samples all computed the same results.
Each produced sample keeps a digest of what its raw computed, every plot's
variables and points and nothing else of its headers, a sampled complex axis
by its real part since ngspice writes the imaginary part of an AC frequency
from memory it never set. An `identical_samples` observation names each
`assign` point where every sample compared there computed one result, and
leads with "All samples of …" only when every sample of every point ran and
each point computed one result. Their draws changed nothing the simulator
saved: a rule that reaches no part the deck saves, or a PDK whose statistical
models never switched on. A sample with no digest, recorded before this
version or with a raw the reader cannot walk to its end, is counted in a
`samples_not_compared` observation instead of being left out.
