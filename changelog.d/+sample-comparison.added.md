A Monte Carlo receipt says when its samples computed the same results. Each
produced sample keeps a digest of what its raw computed, every plot's variables
and points and nothing else of its headers, a complex axis by its real part
since ngspice writes the imaginary part of an AC frequency from memory it
never set. At each `assign` point, samples that share a digest are reported in
an `identical_samples` observation naming the point and how many of its
samples differ; it leads with "All samples of …" only when every sample of
every point ran and each point computed one result. Their draws changed
nothing the simulator saved: a rule that reaches no part the deck saves, or a
PDK whose statistical models never switched on. A sample with no digest,
recorded before this version or with a raw the reader cannot walk to its end,
is counted in a `samples_not_compared` observation instead of being left out.
