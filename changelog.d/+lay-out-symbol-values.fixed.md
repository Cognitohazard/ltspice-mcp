The layout check and the render now lay out a value a part takes from its symbol, such as a transistor's NPN or a MOSFET's NMOS when the part gives no value of its own, where LTspice draws it. Such text drawn across a wire, a ground, another part or other text is now reported. A net label or a ground on a part's pin with no wire is now drawn out of the part, on the side its pin faces, as LTspice draws it. Before, a label's text was centred above the pin and a ground pointed down, so a sheet with a flag on every pin got reports of overlaps LTspice does not draw and missed some it does. A pin's `dir` in `edit_schematic` and `inspect` replies now names that same side: a transistor's drain, source, collector or emitter at a corner of its symbol faces up or down, where it was given as left or right.
