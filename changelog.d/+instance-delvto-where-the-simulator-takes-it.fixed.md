A per-instance `delvto` or `mulu0`, written as `X1:delvto` or as an `instances` target with `attribute: "parameter"`, runs only where the simulator is known to apply it to the device's model. On LTspice that is `delvto` on a level 54 (BSIM4) model: LTspice 26 stops on `delvto` on BSIM3 and on `mulu0` on BSIM3 and BSIM4, and LTspice XVII ignores them, so those are now refused with `instance_parameter_not_taken` before any case runs, and any other level with `instance_parameter_unestablished`. On ngspice a model other than BSIM3 or BSIM4 is refused with `unsupported_model` instead of stopping the run. Each refusal names a `mismatch` rule with an `instance` list as the alternative. Requests that ran before on those models are now refused.
