An observation that qualifies a recipe's number now reaches an `analyze_results` reply that leaves the row's value out, once, at the top level: `signal_stats` says a signal is constant over its window, and `ac_structure` says when its corners merged, its phase unwrap was doubtful, or its fit's order disagrees with the high-frequency slope. Each `ac_structure` note now carries a `kind` (`description` for how the structure was read). With the value shown, such an observation is no longer given both in the value and at the top level.
