`inspect(kind="hierarchy")` reports in `profile.names_read_as` which recorded
LTspice build its names inside subcircuits are spelled for, and in
`profile.names_note` why, when the build has no recording of its own (LTspice
IV, 24, 25, one after 26, or one no run has named yet). A run whose raw names
subcircuit members otherwise than its executable was read to carries a
`raw_names_disagree` observation.
