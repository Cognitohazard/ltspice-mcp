A job run with `execution.recoverable` no longer stalls the server when its launches outnumber the threads the server hands file work to. Each launch waited for its launch record to be written while holding one of those threads, and the record needed one of them too. With one job, the extra launches gave up after ten seconds and their cases failed with `submission_failed`. With two or more recoverable jobs at once, on one simulator or several, and sooner on a machine with few cores, every launch could wait for ever: the jobs never finished, `jobs(action="cancel")` could not end them, and every other read the server hands to a thread stalled with them. Launches now run on threads of their own. A cancel, the job's deadline or a shutdown now ends a launch that is still waiting for its record. Its simulator is not started, and the case reports the stop, saying the launch was being recorded.
