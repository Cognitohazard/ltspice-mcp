`verify_circuit`'s `structural_diff` mode, the export's `diff_vs_prior` and `edit_schematic`'s reference check in that mode compared every element by its bare reference, so two elements of one name in different subcircuits (a top-level `R1` and a block's `R1`, common in an exported hierarchical sheet) were read as one: a real change could be reported as none, a block's change shown as the top level's, and moving a `.subckt` block reported as a change. Each element and directive is now compared in its scope, an entry inside a definition says which (`(in .subckt NAME)`, or a `subckt` field), and a `.param` moved between a body and the top level is a difference. A new `not_compared` list says what the delta did not compare: connections in `structural_diff` mode, a subcircuit defined more than once in one scope (whose comparison then gives `equivalent: null`), and included files' cards. `diff_vs_prior` now also lists each pin moved to another net (`connections_changed`), and is an empty object rather than absent when nothing changed since the previous export.
