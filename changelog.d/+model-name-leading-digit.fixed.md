A model swap in `run_experiments` refused a model name that starts with a digit, such as `2N5486` or `1N4148`: a `REF@model` assignment failed each case at submission, and the `instances` form refused it at input. Both forms now accept any name a `.model` card can declare. They and a Monte Carlo `rule: "model"` target refuse at input, naming the fix, a text that is no model name: empty, or holding a space, quote, comma, `=`, `;`, brace or parenthesis, or a lone `$`.
