On QSPICE and Xyce, a `mismatch` rule that reaches a top-level MOSFET whose W, L or m is an expression or missing is refused with `mismatch_geometry_unresolved`, naming each such device and why. Before, the device was skipped and the draw was made for the other devices alone, so the spread reported covered fewer devices than the rule named. A rule that ran before on such a deck is now refused: write W, L and m as numbers, or narrow the prefix.
